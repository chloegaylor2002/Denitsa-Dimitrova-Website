Research

My work explores how RF technologies can be designed for both sustainability and accessibility, with applications ranging from biodegradable electronics to energy harvesting systems.

Research Areas
Biodegradable RF Devices

I design RF components using materials that reduce long-term environmental impact while addressing electronic waste challenges.

RF Energy Harvesting

My work develops circuits and systems that convert ambient RF signals into usable electrical power for low-energy devices.

RF for Underserved Communities

I am interested in how RF technologies can be deployed in resource-constrained environments to expand access to energy and communication systems.

Research Overview

This framework guides my approach to designing RF systems that are energy-efficient, sustainable, and deployable in real-world contexts.

Current Work
Biodegradable Antenna Design

Designing and testing RF antennas using biodegradable substrates for environmentally sustainable applications.

Low-Power Energy Harvesting Systems

Developing circuits capable of capturing and storing ambient RF energy for use in low-power devices.

Applications in Resource-Constrained Environments

Exploring how RF systems can be adapted for use in communities with limited infrastructure.

I aim to build RF systems that are not only high-performing, but also practical for broader environmental and societal use.
