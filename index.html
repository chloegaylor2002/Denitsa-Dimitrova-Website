Denitsa Dimitrova

Ph.D. Student in Electrical Engineering

Georgia Institute of Technology
ATHENA Lab

My research focuses on biodegradable RF device design and RF energy harvesting, with an emphasis on sustainable wireless technologies and practical deployment in underserved communities.

I am interested in bridging fundamental RF engineering with real-world systems that are environmentally responsible, energy-aware, and socially impactful.

Research Areas
Biodegradable RF Devices

Designing RF components with sustainable materials to reduce electronic waste.

RF Energy Harvesting

Capturing ambient RF signals to power low-energy devices without traditional batteries.

RF for Underserved Communities

Developing wireless technologies that expand access to energy and communication systems.

I work at the intersection of sustainability, energy, and RF systems to design technologies that are both technically robust and socially impactful.
